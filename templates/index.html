About EcoCycle

Pioneering Responsible Electronics Recycling

India is currently the third-largest generator of electronic waste globally. Over 90% of discarded devices end up in the informal sector, where hazardous open burning and crude acid leaching poison ground soil, air, and drinking water aquifers.

EcoCycle bridges the critical gap between citizens, institutions, and government-authorized recyclers. We provide a fully digital, transparent platform ensuring every discarded device is safely collected, documented, and scientifically recovered according to India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022.

Equipment Categories

Electronics We Accept & Recycle

Explore accepted device types and access step-by-step citizen pre-disposal guidance for each category.

Mobile Phones & Tablets
Smartphones, basic phones, tablets

High concentration of precious metals (gold, silver, palladium). Requires factory cryptographic reset and SIM/SD card removal before recycling.

Read Disposal Guide
Laptops & Computers
Desktops, servers, motherboards

Complex assemblies containing printed circuit boards and lithium batteries. Demands certified data sanitization and separate component dismantling.

Read Disposal Guide
Batteries
Lithium-ion, Lead-acid, NiMH cells

High risk of thermal runaway fires if compacted in garbage trucks. Must be insulated with non-conductive tape on terminals and kept in cool, dry storage.

Read Disposal Guide
Chargers & Cables
Power cords, adapters, copper cables

Rich in high-purity copper and recyclable PVC. Open burning releases deadly carcinogenic dioxins; authorized recyclers use zero-emission mechanical granulators.

Read Disposal Guide
Televisions & Monitors
LED, LCD, OLED, and CRT screens

Contain fluorescent lamps with mercury vapors or CRT leaded glass. Must never be broken or shattered prior to controlled specialized extraction.

Read Disposal Guide
Household Appliances
Microwaves, printers, electronics

Contain motors, transformers, capacitors, and engineering plastics. Fully dismantleable for material recovery and circular remanufacturing.
