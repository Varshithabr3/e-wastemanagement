Platform Capabilities

Three Fundamental Project Objectives

Designed in compliance with academic requirements and real-world environmental protocols.

1. Digital E-Waste Registration & Requests

Citizens can catalog unwanted mobile phones, laptops, batteries, and appliances with condition notes and photos, then submit digital doorstep pickup or drop-off collection requests.

  • Multi-category inventory logging
  • Unique Trackable Request ID
  • Downloadable / Printable formal receipt

2. Authorized Centres & Disposal Guidance

Dedicated module connecting users with verified recyclers authorized by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB).

  • Official government source citations
  • Clear verified vs demo badge distinction
  • Category-wise step-by-step disposal guides

3. Admin Dashboard & Public Awareness

Robust administrator management portal featuring visual Chart.js analytics, request review controls, and an educational hub with interactive environmental quiz.

  • Interactive Chart.js data visualizations
  • Centre & guideline CRUD operations
  • Gamified public awareness quiz with certificate
