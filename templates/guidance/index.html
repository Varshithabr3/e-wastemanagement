Proper E-Waste Disposal & Safe Storage Guidelines

Scientific, step-by-step instructions for citizens and institutions to responsibly prepare electronic devices for recycling, prevent catastrophic fires, and protect personal data.

Authorized Centres Directory
Never in Household Dustbins

Electronic items in regular landfills produce toxic leachate that contaminates aquifers and sparks high-temperature lithium fires in compaction garbage trucks.

Safe Pre-Disposal Storage

Store unused gadgets in a cool, dry area away from direct sunlight. Always tape battery terminals with clear electrical tape to prevent short circuits.

3R Hierarchy (Reuse & Recycle)

Working devices should first be refurbished or donated. Non-functional electronics must be channeled strictly to registered CPCB/KSPCB hydrometallurgical recyclers.
