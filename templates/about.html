About the E-Waste Management System

A software-based digital solution engineered for academic evaluation in Computer Science & Environmental Science (Course Code: BCSP508 - CSE).

The exponential proliferation of consumer electronic devices has turned electrical and electronic waste into the world's fastest-growing municipal waste stream. When discarded casually with regular household trash, toxic heavy metals such as lead, mercury, and cadmium contaminate groundwater and soil. This application addresses this challenge by providing citizens with transparent recycling pathways and educating the community on scientific electronic disposal.

Academic Scope Notice

Notice for Evaluators and Visitors: This application is a purely software-based academic project prototype.

  • Does NOT require physical IoT devices, microcontrollers, or physical sensors.
  • Does NOT use physical smart bins or automated weighing hardware.
  • Does NOT conduct actual physical transport or physical electronic waste pickup.
  • All registration, collection requests, status updates, tracking, and receipts are handled digitally and software-simulated for demonstration.
Department of Computer Science & Engineering

Project Objectives & Scope

Objective 1: User Platform

To build an intuitive, secure web portal where users can register electronic waste details, upload photos, specify conditions, and digitally submit collection requests with unique tracking codes.

Objective 2: Authorized Centres & Guidance

To curate authentic, officially verified information from statutory pollution boards (CPCB, KSPCB), guide citizens on safe pre-disposal storage, data sanitization, and provide Google Maps directions.

Objective 3: Administrative Dashboard

To empower administrators with data-driven analytics via Chart.js, workflow approval controls for collection requests, inventory filtering, and full management over authorized recycling centre listings.

Technology Stack Architecture

Python Flask

Lightweight, WSGI compliant microframework powering RESTful routing, session auth, and business logic.

MySQL 8.0 & PyMySQL

Relational database storing user credentials, e-waste inventories, verified centres, and request workflows.

Bootstrap 5

Modern, fully responsive frontend grid and component framework with mobile-first UI design.

Chart.js & Font Awesome

Interactive visual charts for category statistics and clean vector iconography across all modules.

Commitment to Official Data Integrity

In strict compliance with academic standards, all authorized recycling centres featured in this portal are sourced directly from official public directories of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and the Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) under the E-Waste (Management) Rules. Any mock demonstration data is unambiguously marked with a "Demonstration Data" badge.
