EcoCycle Environmental Initiative

About the E-Waste Management System

EcoCycle is a public environmental service platform dedicated to advancing responsible electronic waste collection, scientific recycling, and circular resource recovery across India.

The exponential proliferation of consumer electronics and computing hardware has turned electrical and electronic equipment (EEE) waste into one of the world's fastest-growing environmental crises. When discarded into municipal dustbins, toxic heavy metals such as lead, mercury, cadmium, and brominated flame retardants leach into soil and municipal water tables. EcoCycle provides citizens, institutions, and enterprises with a transparent, certified digital pipeline to channel end-of-life electronics directly to statutory authorized recyclers.

Our Core Commitments
Standards of Operation
  • Zero-Landfill Protocol: Ensuring all collected equipment undergoes scientific segregation, material reclamation, and environmentally sound treatment.
  • Statutory Verification: Partnering exclusively with recyclers, dismantlers, and refurbishers authorized by CPCB & KSPCB.
  • Certified Data Sanitization: Providing verified data erasure protocols to prevent personal and institutional data compromise.
  • Digital Manifests: Offering transparent tracking IDs and verifiable digital collection receipts for every transaction.

Platform Capabilities

Our Three Strategic Pillars

Engineered to make responsible electronic disposal transparent, accessible, and measurable.

1. Citizen Platform & Logistics

A streamlined web interface allowing users to catalog electronic waste, specify device condition, choose doorstep pickup or drop-off preferences, and generate official digital disposal manifests with real-time tracking codes.

2. Authorized Directory & Guidance

A verified directory of certified facilities licensed by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB), complemented by scientific step-by-step citizen pre-disposal preparation protocols.

3. Analytics & Public Education

Administrative intelligence featuring real-time Chart.js volume metrics, status workflow audits, and interactive public awareness modules with an eco-recognition evaluation to educate communities on hazardous materials.

Statutory Compliance & Regulatory Framework

MoEFCC Regulations

Operates strictly within the mandate of the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 issued by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India.

CPCB EPR Portal

Supports Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) mandates, ensuring end-of-life electronics are accounted for and channelized only to registered recyclers.

KSPCB State Standards

Adheres to Karnataka State Pollution Control Board guidelines, monitoring local recycling capacities, environmental emission thresholds, and safe logistics channelization.
